Methodology

What It Measures

Rates bushfire risk on a 0-100 scale (100 = safest). Combines official planning overlays with satellite vegetation analysis, terrain slope, and fire history.

Data Sources

  • Planning overlays (VIC BMO, NSW BPL, WA OBRM, SA zones, TAS)
  • ESA WorldCover 10m land cover (vegetation fuel load)
  • Terrain slope: GA 5m LiDAR bare-earth where covered, 30m DEM elsewhere
  • MODIS Burned Area (fire history, 3 seasons)

Scoring

  1. Official overlay severity (extreme/high/moderate/low)
  2. Vegetation fuel risk (tree cover > shrub > grass > built-up)
  3. Terrain slope amplifier (steep = faster fire spread)
  4. Fire history penalty (recent burns = proven risk)
  5. Final score = min(overlay, satellite) with fire adjustment

Limitations

  • Not a BAL (Bushfire Attack Level) assessment
  • QLD, NT lack public overlay data
  • MODIS 500m resolution misses small fires
