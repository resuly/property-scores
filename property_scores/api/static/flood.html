Methodology

What It Measures

Rates flood risk on a 0-100 scale (100 = safest) as a relative screening signal across all Australian states. It combines four independent data layers into directional guidance from open data and terrain physics, not a determination-grade or insurance-pricing assessment.

Data Sources

  • ArcGIS Planning Overlays (VIC/NSW/SA/TAS/ACT)
  • JRC Global Surface Water, 38-year satellite record
  • HAND (Height Above Nearest Drainage): LiDAR bare-earth where covered (NSW/QLD 5m raster, VIC/TAS contours), else GA DEM-H 30m
  • ERA5 P95 extreme rainfall climatology

Scoring

  1. Official overlay check (Floodway/LSIO/SBO zones)
  2. JRC satellite flood occurrence in 500m radius
  3. HAND elevation modifier (physical vulnerability)
  4. ERA5 extreme rainfall compound risk
  5. Final score = min(overlay, JRC) adjusted by HAND

Limitations

  • QLD, WA, NT lack public ArcGIS overlay data (satellite and terrain signals still apply)
  • JRC 30m resolution may miss localized drainage
  • Elevation is a 30m grid (~1-3m vertical uncertainty); terrain-based risk is a directional screening signal, not survey grade
  • Not FEMA/BFE determination-grade or an insurance-pricing input, and not a substitute for a professional flood assessment
